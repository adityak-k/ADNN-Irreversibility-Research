# Verified dataset v2

`irreversibility_dataset_v2.jsonl` contains 2,400 examples generated by executing fresh `Sandbox` instances. The earlier `irreversibility_dataset.jsonl` and `generate_dataset.py` remain available unchanged for comparison. The approved rules in `../REVERSIBILITY_RULES.md` were not changed for this dataset.

## Generation and labels

`generate_verified_dataset.py` creates 600 examples for each of the four actions and 800 for each of the three labels. For each record it configures a new sandbox, captures the action-specific initial operational state, executes the candidate action, attempts available recovery tools up to the three-call horizon, captures the final operational state, and derives the label from exact state equality plus recorded meaningful recovery progress. The sandbox's audit history is stored separately from those operational snapshots. The generator uses seed `20261118` for repeatability.

Conditions cover current, stale, and missing file recovery material; email recall, correction, and unavailable recovery; and pending-cancellable, settled-refundable, and settled-no-refund transfers. For pending transfers, the sender is debited to reserve funds and the recipient is credited only at settlement. For settled transfers, the settlement commitment remains after a refund as specified by the approved rules.

## Pairing and diversity

Each action has 60 matched pairs for each of the three label comparisons (reversible/partial, partial/irreversible, reversible/irreversible). Across four actions this is 720 pairs and 1,440 paired examples, or 60% of the dataset. Within each pair, scenario details and action parameters are held fixed except for one controlled recovery-condition field. Remaining single examples complete an exactly balanced action-by-label design.

Scenario parameters vary across file names and contents, backup age, email content and recipients, and transfer values and account balances. State descriptions use varied natural-language forms and omit explicit class names. They describe the state before the candidate action.

## Record use

Core fields (`id`, `environment`, `state_text`, `action_text`, `label`, `horizon`, `source`, `pair_id`) are retained. `scenario_id` and `pair_role` identify matched scenarios. `action_parameters` records the pre-action conditions and candidate operation. `state_phase` is `pre_action` for every record. In a later model experiment, use `state_text` and `action_text` as the agent observation and candidate action; do not feed the fields listed in `evaluation_only_fields` to the model. Those fields—initial/final operational snapshots, recovery outcomes and traces, lifecycle state, and audit history—are ground-truth evidence for analysis and validation, including information obtained after acting.

## Validation

`validate_verified_dataset.py` validates schema, class/action balance, IDs, pairs, pair condition controls, duplicate and conflicting scenarios, explicit label wording, horizon, and operational classification. It then replays all 2,400 scenarios against fresh sandboxes and compares labels, initial/final operational state, recovery actions, execution trace, lifecycle, and audit history. The validation report is saved at `../results/logs/dataset_v2_validation.txt`.

No train/test split is created. Scenario-level splitting remains a separate research-design step.
